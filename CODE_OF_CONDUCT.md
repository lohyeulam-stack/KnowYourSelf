# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want KnowYourSelf to be a practical, respectful space for people experimenting with AI-assisted work, knowledge management, product engineering, and human-in-the-loop systems.

Participants should:

- be respectful and constructive;
- challenge ideas rather than people;
- provide evidence when making strong technical or architectural claims;
- distinguish facts, hypotheses, recommendations, and personal preferences;
- avoid sharing confidential information about employers, customers, or other people;
- welcome correction and update conclusions when evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- deliberately exposing private or confidential information;
- malicious manipulation of project history or attribution;
- presenting private company practices or data as public examples without authorization.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in loss of participation privileges.

Please report serious conduct issues privately through the repository's available GitHub contact or moderation mechanisms rather than escalating them in a public thread.
